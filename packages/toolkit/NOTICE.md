# Data and third-party notices

The bundled world data is derived from Natural Earth 1:110m country boundaries.
Natural Earth data is in the public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/.

The JavaScript bundle includes d3-geo, d3-geo-projection, d3-array, and their transitive dependencies. Their license notices are included in `dist/THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.txt` by the build.
